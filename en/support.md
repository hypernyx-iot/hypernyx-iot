<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hypernyx Support</h1>

Need help with the **Hypernyx** app? We're here to help.

## Contact Us

For questions, technical issues, feedback, or any other request, email our support team:

**<support@hypernyx.com>**

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

- A short description of the problem
- Your device model and operating system version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 17.5)
- The Hypernyx app version
- Screenshots, if applicable

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open the side menu, go to **Profile Settings**, and tap **Delete Profile**. See the step-by-step guide on the [Delete Account](/en/delete-account/) page.

### What happens to my data when I delete my account?

Deleting your profile permanently removes all your personal data, including your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, and added devices.

### How is my personal information handled?

Please read our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy/). For privacy-related questions, you can also write to <privacy@hypernyx.com>.

### Where can I find the terms of use?

See our [Terms & Conditions](/en/terms-and-conditions/).
